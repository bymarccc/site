Pay in 2 — Agreement

This page explains the terms of bymarccc's own 2-payment option at checkout. It is a payment plan offered directly by bymarccc through your card issuer — it is not provided, processed or guaranteed by PayPal or any other third party.

Key information
Number of payments2 — 50% today, 50% in 30 days
Interest rate0% — fixed for the duration of the plan
FeesNone. You are charged exactly the order total, split into two equal parts.
Payment methodCard only (the same card used for your first payment)
Second paymentCharged automatically, 30 days after your order date
Providerbymarccc, via our card payment processor

How it works

When you choose "Make 2 payments" at checkout, your card is charged for the first half of the order total at the time of purchase. The remaining half is charged automatically to the same card 30 days later. You will not be asked to approve the second charge separately — by completing checkout with this option selected, you authorize both charges.

Eligibility

  • Available on card payments only; not available for cash on delivery.
  • Both charges are billed to the same card used for the first payment.
  • Available only where offered at checkout for your order.

If a payment fails

If the second charge cannot be completed (for example, insufficient funds or an expired card), we will attempt to contact you to arrange payment by another method. Your order and any custom production may be paused until the balance is settled.

Returns & cancellations

Returns and cancellations follow bymarccc's standard policy for the product purchased. If an eligible return is approved before the second payment is due, the second charge will not be collected; if it has already been collected, it will be refunded along with the rest of the order.

Questions about an order paid in 2 instalments? Contact bymarccc support with your order number and we'll help directly — there is no separate PayPal or third-party account involved in this plan.
